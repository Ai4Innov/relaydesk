# Code of Conduct

Relay Desk adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct.

## Our pledge

We, as members, contributors and maintainers, pledge to make participation in this community a harassment-free experience for everyone. This applies regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation. We act in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate, and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for users and the community.
- Protect privacy. Never post other people's personal data or private communications, including in bug reports.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory or discriminatory comments.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in all project spaces, such as issues, pull requests and discussions, and wherever someone represents the project in public.

Report incidents to the maintainers at **[contact@ai4innov.tech](mailto:contact@ai4innov.tech)**. All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, and may temporarily or permanently ban participants who violate this code. Enforcement follows the [Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
